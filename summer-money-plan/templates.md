## 4. Walk-in script (with demo on your phone, parent nearby)

> "Hi, do you have a minute? I'm a student and I build websites for local businesses. I actually already made a demo for [business name] — here, this is what it'd look like *(hand them the phone)*. If you like it, it's €250 one-time and I can have it live this week. And for €25 a month I keep it updated so you never think about it."

Objection answers:
- *"We already have a guy."* → "No problem! If he's ever slow or expensive, here's my number. The demo stays up this month."
- *"We don't need a website."* → "Totally fair — most of your customers Google you first though. Can I at least fix up your Google Business Profile? €50, done tomorrow."
- *"Too expensive."* → "I can do a one-page version for €150. Same look, just simpler."
