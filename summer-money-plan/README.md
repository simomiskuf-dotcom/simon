## Step 0 — The honest part first (read this, don't skip it)

Three facts that shape everything:

1. **Anthropic's terms require users to be 18+.** There is no parent-account exception for the *user*. The compliant setup: **a parent owns the Claude account and works with you** — you design, they drive / supervise sessions together. Treat Claude Code as a family tool, not your personal login. (Also: GitHub + GitHub Copilot are 13+, and Copilot is **free for students** with agent mode — that's the AI coding tool you can legally hold in your own name. Use both.)
2. **Almost every money platform needs a parent on the account.** Fiverr officially supports 13–17 year olds *on a parent-owned account*. PayPal, Stripe, payouts everywhere — parent's name. This isn't a workaround; it's literally the supported path. Get your parent on board in Week 1 or nothing else works.
3. **In Slovakia you can't legally invoice until 18** (živnosť requires 18). The clean model: parent contracts/invoices, you do the work, money lands in parent's account → transferred to yours. Under **€2,876.90/year** total income, no Slovak tax return is even needed.

Full details + the parent checklist: [money-and-safety.md](money-and-safety.md).

### Pillar 1 — Minecraft Bedrock addon commissions 🎮 (your unfair advantage)

You already write working `@minecraft/server` scripting code. People pay real money for exactly this:

- **What it pays:** small script features €20–€75, full custom packs (airdrop/quest-system scale) €100–€500. Fiverr's Bedrock addon gigs run $10 (simple) to $250+ (complex). Realm/SMP owners pay flat €20–€150 for custom features.
- **It's 100% legal:** Mojang's EULA explicitly allows *selling your time* (commissions). What's NOT allowed: selling pre-built addons directly to the public outside the official Marketplace, or paywalling downloads on Patreon.
- **Where the clients are:**
  1. **Discord** — Bedrock Add-Ons community, freelance/hiring servers (find via disboard.org tags "hiring"/"commissions"). This is where most real commission flow happens.
  2. **BuiltByBit** (builtbybit.com) — 13+ with parental permission and supervision; active Bedrock tags + Services/Hiring forum.
  3. **Fiverr** — parent-owned account, gig: "I will create a custom Minecraft Bedrock behavior pack."
- **Portfolio move (Week 1):** polish the airdrop + SMP quest packs, release them **free** on MCPEDL + CurseForge (parent-held account for the rewards program — it pays a little: ~€5–50/mo at big download counts, but mainly it's your shop window), and make a 60-second showcase video of each.
- **The ceiling-raiser:** contract for an existing **Minecraft Marketplace partner studio** (e.g., Waypoint Studios, Pathway Studio recruit freelance Bedrock devs). That's the foot in the door to the official Marketplace, where add-ons like *Actions & Stuff* have made hundreds of thousands of dollars. Full partnership needs 18+ + a business — that's your long game.

### Pillar 2 — Local business websites 🏪 (best €-per-project)

Claude Code turns a week of web work into an afternoon. Small businesses pay €200–€500 for exactly what you can build, and agencies charge them €1,500–€8,000.

- **The offer:** one-page or small site for a restaurant / barbershop / tutor / tradesperson: €200–€400 flat, plus **€20–€40/month** for "I handle everything" (updates, Google Business Profile, menu changes). **The retainer is the real business** — AI site builders are cheap, but the owner doesn't want to touch them. You're selling "you never have to think about this."
- **How you actually get clients** (in order of what works):
  1. Family network — parents' coworkers, coaches, relatives' businesses. Warm referrals close; cold ones mostly don't.
  2. Walk in with a **demo you already built for their specific business** on your phone. "I already made this for you" beats any pitch.
  3. Only then: local Facebook groups / cold messages.
- **Rules:** 50% deposit up front, one-page signed agreement with a parent's name on it, parent present at any in-person meeting with a stranger.

### Pillar 3 — Teach kids coding + AI tools 🧑‍🏫 (the reliable floor)

The least glamorous, most dependable money: **€15–€25/hour** teaching younger kids Scratch, Python basics, Minecraft addon making, or "how to use AI tools." Market rate for coding tutors runs $14–$74/hr. Two or three weekly students = €100+/week, guaranteed, no clients to chase. Sell it through parents' networks and school contacts. Bonus: "learn to make Minecraft mods" is an easy sell to kids — and you demo with your own packs.

### The long game (runs in the background, costs ~1 day/week)

- **Content as a funnel, not income:** short YouTube/TikTok clips — "I built an airdrop system for Minecraft Bedrock," "I built a website for a local barbershop in one day with AI." Monetization is 18+ and takes 6–15 months anyway; the point is *clients and downloads find you*.
- **One digital product bet:** a polished addon template/boilerplate or a Framer template (new accepted creators reportedly earn $500–$2,000/template, Framer takes 0%). Low odds, but it keeps paying after summer.
- **At 18:** transfer the Fiverr account to your name, register živnosť, apply to the Minecraft Marketplace Partner Program (needs 18+, business entity, 3+ polished published works — which this summer builds).

## Rules that keep the money (memorize these)

1. **Never deliver full work before full payment.** 50% up front, watermarked/limited preview, rest before handoff.
2. **Stay on-platform** where escrow exists. Anyone pushing to move off-platform or pay via crypto/Western Union/"Friends & Family" = scam.
3. **Overpayment = scam.** "I sent too much, refund the difference" is the #1 freelancer scam. Never refund before funds fully clear.
4. **Unsolicited Discord DMs offering paid work are presumed scams** (fake recruiters, "test my beta" token stealers). You find clients; clients who find *you* out of nowhere get maximum suspicion.
5. **Never send ID, selfies, or logins to a client.** Only parents do KYC, only in official platform flows.
6. **Include Mojang's disclaimer** on anything Minecraft: "NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT PRODUCT. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT."
7. **Ignore "$10k/month vibe coding" content.** Those people make money selling courses, not doing the work. Your numbers above are the real ones.
