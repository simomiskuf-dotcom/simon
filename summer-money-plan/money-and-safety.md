# 🛡️ Money & Safety — Parent Setup, Getting Paid, Taxes, Scam Defense

This is the unglamorous file that keeps the money and keeps you out of trouble. **A parent should read this file too.**

---

## Parent setup checklist (Week 1 — nothing works without this)

- [ ] **Claude account:** Anthropic's terms are 18+ for users, no minor exception. The parent owns the account and supervises/participates in its use — treat it as working *together*. The teen's own legal AI tool: **GitHub (13+) + free student GitHub Copilot** via GitHub Education.
- [ ] **Fiverr:** parent creates and manages the account (Fiverr's official Minor Policy, June 2025, supports exactly this for ages 13–17; the gig must state work is by a minor under guardian supervision; at 18 it transfers to the teen).
- [ ] **PayPal:** parent's account only — PayPal is 18+ everywhere, and detected minor accounts get closed with funds frozen up to 180 days. Never risk it.
- [ ] **BuiltByBit / Polymart:** 13+ allowed *with parental permission and supervision* per their ToS. Payouts flow through parent's PayPal.
- [ ] **CurseForge Rewards / MCPEDL:** rewards contract is 18+ → parent-held. (1 point = $0.05, cashout from $5; expect €5–50/mo only at large download counts — it's portfolio money, not salary.)
- [ ] **Bank:** Slovakia — from age 15 with an ID (občiansky preukaz), the teen can open a student account alone, free at most banks (Tatra banka, SLSP, VÚB, ČSOB, Prima). Under 15: parent opens a children's account. Alternative: Revolut <18 (requires parent with Revolut).
- [ ] **Contracts & invoicing:** in Slovakia a minor cannot hold a živnosť (18+, no exceptions) — regular freelancing in the teen's own name is legally not possible and unregistered business can even be a criminal offence. **The parent signs agreements and invoices clients**; the work is the teen's, the legal counterparty is the parent.
- [ ] **Avoid entirely (ToS bans minors, no parent route):** Upwork, Ko-fi, Payhip, teen-owned PayPal.

## Taxes (plain language, Slovakia, 2025 rules — parent verifies current year)

- Total taxable income **≤ €2,876.90/year → no tax return needed at all.** A normal summer at €1,000–2,500 stays under this.
- The €500 "occasional income" exemption is narrow and does **not** cover repeated freelance gigs — don't rely on it.
- If the parent invoices clients, that income is legally the parent's — a parent + accountant question once amounts get serious.
- From 15 + finished elementary school, a **dohoda o brigádnickej práci študentov** is the legal route if a local company wants to formally employ the teen (max ~20h/week avg, levy exemption up to €200/month).

## Getting paid — the flow

```
Client → (platform escrow or invoice) → parent's PayPal/bank → transfer → teen's student account
```

- **On-platform (Fiverr/BuiltByBit):** platform holds escrow; Fiverr takes 20% and holds funds 14 days — price that in.
- **Direct clients (local businesses, Discord):** 50% deposit before starting → watermarked/limited preview → final 50% → full delivery. Bank transfer to parent's account beats everything for local clients (no fees, no chargebacks).
- **Never:** deliver first and "get paid after," accept PayPal Friends & Family (no protection + ToS violation), accept crypto/gift cards/Western Union.

## Scam defense — the patterns, memorized

| Scam | How it looks | Defense |
|---|---|---|
| **Overpayment** | "I sent €2,000 instead of €200, refund the difference" | The original payment is fake/stolen and will bounce. Never refund anything before funds fully clear (days, not minutes). |
| **Off-platform pull** | "Let's do this outside Fiverr, cheaper for both" | Kills escrow protection — that's the point. Stay on-platform with strangers. |
| **Fake recruiter DM** | Unsolicited Discord DM: "we saw your work, paid position" | Unsolicited paid offers to young devs are presumed scams. You applied to nothing = it's nothing. Verify via official servers only. |
| **"Test my game/beta"** | A "client" sends an .exe or link to try | Token stealer / malware. Never run client files. Your work runs in Minecraft, not their launcher. |
| **Chargeback fraud** | Card/PayPal payment reversed after delivery | Deposits up front, signed agreement (parent's name), bank transfer for locals. |
| **Free work / "trial task"** | "Do a sample first, then we'll pay for the rest" | Portfolio pieces are public; trial tasks for a specific client are paid. Point at MCPEDL and decline. |
| **Fake escrow site** | "Let's use this safe middleman site" (their link) | Only real platform escrow counts. Any escrow site a client links is theirs. |

## Personal safety rules (non-negotiable)

1. Never send ID documents, selfies, or account logins to anyone. Only parents do identity verification, only inside official platform flows.
2. Parent present at any in-person meeting with someone the family doesn't know. Public places only.
3. Real name/address/school stays private with online clients — first name and portfolio links are enough.
4. A parent can read any client conversation at any time. Weird vibes → show a parent *before* replying.
5. Client relationships live in writing (Discord/email). Calls with strangers: no.

## Minecraft legal rules (so you never get burned)

- ✅ **Commissions are explicitly EULA-allowed** — Mojang: paying you to make a mod = "paying for your time."
- ✅ Free distribution + ad/reward revenue + donations for free content.
- ✅ Selling on the official Marketplace (via a partner studio now; own partnership at 18 — needs business entity + 3 published works).
- ❌ Selling pre-built addons to the public outside the Marketplace.
- ❌/⚠️ Patreon-paywalled addon downloads — gray-to-violating; keep downloads free, monetize commissions and tips.
- ❌ Using Mojang assets commercially; "Minecraft" as the primary name of your product/brand.
- Always include: *"NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT PRODUCT. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT."*
